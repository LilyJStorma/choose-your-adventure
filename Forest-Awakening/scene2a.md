## Scene Two: Where are you?

You lift your head slightly to get a better look at your surroundings. The first thing you note is a lanky looking man leaning against a tree a few feet away from where you sit. A strange looking mask covers his face and loose baggy clothing covers the rest of him.
Other than him there’s what appears to be a large wooden crate off to the side and a large briefcase resting at the man's feet.
If he’s noticed that you’re awake, he doesn’t say anything.
