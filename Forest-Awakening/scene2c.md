## Scene Two: Escape attempt
You try to lift yourself to your feet, swaying as you do so. Though you are able to stand upright the moment you start to try and sprint you fall with a solid *thud*. As you fall your head seems to hit an invisible wall, one made by the flower circle.
At the noise you’ve made you hear a startled voice

> “Wha-? Oh! You! You’re uhm, awake…?”

He sounds confused and a little alarmed.
